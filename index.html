How the scoring works

The 2025 AP Physics 1 exam (revised format) uses a 100-point composite:

  • MCQ composite = (correct / 40) × 50
  • FRQ composite = (% credit / 100) × 50
  • Total = MCQ composite + FRQ composite

Both sections are weighted equally at 50% each. The four FRQ questions have raw point values of 10, 12, 10, and 8 (40 total), all scaled to 50 composite points.
